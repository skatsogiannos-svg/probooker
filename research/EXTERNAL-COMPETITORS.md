# External (non-Shopify) Competitor Research — live pulls 2026-11-10

## Apps studied
Calendly, Acuity Scheduling (Squarespace), Square Appointments, Mindbody,
Fresha, Booksy, Vagaro, YouCanBookMe, Cal.com, TidyCal.
(Páginas: pricing + feature pages, fetched via curl.)

## Category leaders & positioning
- **Calendly** — sales/meetings, routing (round-robin, collective), workflows,
  embedded scheduling, Salesforce/Marketo integrations. The scheduling UX gold standard.
- **Acuity** — small business: memberships, packages, gift certificates, class
  scheduling, appointment waitlist, up to 36 calendars. Strong feature breadth.
- **Square Appointments** — POS-native: cancellation policy + **no-show fees**,
  multi-staff, waitlist, prepaid class packages, resource management (rooms),
  loyalty + customer directory, contracts. 
- **Mindbody** — studio/gym: memberships, class packs, **AI Concierge** (24/7 front
  desk), lead management dashboard, email/SMS marketing automation, payments-native.
- **Fresha** — salon: **AI Concierge**, upfront payments to cut no-shows, online
  reputation, marketplace of 25M+ customers, 360° insights.
- **Booksy / Vagaro** — barber/salon, marketplace-driven discovery, deposits.
- **Cal.com / TidyCal** — open-source / indie: routing, group, timezone UI, API.
- **YouCanBookMe** — embeddable booking page, routing, workflows, WhatsApp.

## Features present OUTSIDE Shopify that Shopify booking apps largely LACK (opportunity)
1. **No-show fees / cancellation policy enforcement with auto-charge** (Square, Fresha).
   → Our "No-Shield". Big gap on Shopify.
2. **Class & group scheduling with capacity + prepaid class packages** (Acuity, Square, Mindbody).
3. **Memberships / recurring revenue** bundled with booking (Acuity, Mindbody).
4. **Gift certificates** tied to booking (Acuity, Square).
5. **Resource management** (rooms, chairs, equipment), not just staff (Square, Mindbody).
6. **AI Concierge** — a 24/7 booking assistant (Mindbody, Fresha). None of the
   Shopify booking apps have this (only EasyAB's $99 "Claude" tier).
7. **Lead management dashboard** — capture, nurture, convert (Mindbody, Calendly routing).
8. **Marketing automation**: email/SMS campaigns, re-engagement triggers,
   birthday/lapsed-client win-backs (Mindbody, Square).
9. **Routing**: round-robin, collective, priority (Calendly, Cal.com).
10. **Waitlist with auto-notify** on cancellation (Acuity, Square).
11. **90° insights / future bookings report / service cost tracking** (Square, Fresha).
12. **Loyalty programs + customer directory** (Square).
13. **Multi-location management** (all serious players).
14. **Reputation / reviews collection** (Fresha).
15. **Embedded native scheduling page** + API/webhooks/Zapier (Calendly, Cal.com).

## What this means for us
- The Shopify booking apps are ~5 years behind the general scheduling market on
  no-show protection, memberships, AI, marketing, and reputation.
- We can differentiate hard by bringing Square/Fresha/Mindbody-class features
  INTO a Shopify-native, staff-first app.
- Priority innovations to build (see INNOVATIONS.md): No-Shield, AI Concierge
  (waitlist + front-desk assistant), resource management, memberships/packages,
  marketing automation, routing, lead hub, reputation.
