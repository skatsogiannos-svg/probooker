## Verified pricing (USD, from each listing's pricing section)

### Apntly
- Free: Unlimited bookings, email notifications, 1 team member, 1 service.
- Starter $5/mo: Unlimited team members, widget customization, custom email template, reminders, unlimited services.
- Pro $9/mo: + Group appointments, custom questions, calendar integration, Zoom/Google Meet, **Deposits**.
- → Cheap, but feature-gated. Staff is not the primary UX axis.

### Cowlendar (THE revenue-cliff leader)
- Free: 5 bookings/mo.
- Starter $13.99/mo: **Up to $1,000 of booking revenue**.
- Basic $29.99/mo: **Up to $3,000 of booking revenue**.
- Growth $59.99/mo: **Up to $10,000 of booking revenue**.
- → Punishes success: a busy salon hits $10k revenue and must pay $59.99/mo.

### Appointo
- Free → Pro $14/mo → Premium $25/mo → Advanced $39/mo.
- **Hidden**: usage charge for SMS/WhatsApp + **2% charge on subscriptions/bundles** on ALL paid plans.
- → The 2% revenue cut is a real gotcha.

### Easy Appointment Booking
- Free → Standard $15/mo → Pro $29/mo → Pro Plus $39/mo (annual $250/$350 = big annual discount).
- Pro Plus: waitlist, bundles, deposits, POS, **Outlook + Team logins ($8/staff)**.
- **Ultimate $99/mo** required for Claude/AI.
- → Per-staff add-on ($8) + a $99 AI tier.

### Meety (staff-aware but expensive ceiling)
- Free → Basic $7/mo → Starter $14/mo → Business $27/mo → **Enterprise $47/mo**.
- Deposit + Klaviyo + Zapier + API only unlocked at **$47 Enterprise**. ← the "$47 cliff"
- → Best feature depth, but deposit is paywalled at the top tier.

### BookThatApp
- Free: 10 bookings. Lite $25/mo (50 bookings, 1 staff). Premium $49.95/mo (350 bookings, 10 staff). Business $110/mo (1,000 bookings, 20 staff).
- → Aging product (2012), per-booking + per-staff caps, costly above small scale.

## What EVERY competitor has (table stakes — must match)
Appointments, classes, services, rentals, reservations; calendar + scheduling; time slots;
block dates; multi-booking; cancel booking; capacity limits; email + SMS notifications;
multi-language; multi-location; payments/deposits; staff management; custom forms;
custom notifications; branding; widget customization; Google/Outlook calendar sync;
Zoom/Google Meet; cancellation & reschedule flow; reminders.

## What merchants consistently praise (from Shopify Magic review summaries)
- Fast, hands-on, human support (ALL five apps called out for support).
- Easy setup "even for non-techies".
- Flexible booking types (group, multi-day, bundles, subscriptions).
- Deposit / prepayment support.
- Waitlist + automated follow-ups.
- Custom intake questions / waivers.
- Google Calendar / Zoom integration.
- Works with Shopify checkout + Flow.

## Gaps NO competitor closes well (our opportunity)
1. **Nobody is staff-first.** Staff are an admin data field, not the booking hero.
   Customers book "a slot". They do not browse "who you can book with", see photos,
   specialties, or pick a person first. → our core differentiator.
2. **Nobody ships a Staff Dashboard / performance view.** All analytics are
   merchant-level (revenue, bookings). No per-staff no-show rate, revenue,
   repeat-customer %, utilization, or per-staff metrics.
3. **Multi-location is weak/nonexistent** across all five (only tag-level claim).
4. **Every app has a usage cliff** (Cowlendar = revenue, BTA/Meety = bookings/staff,
   Appointo = 2% cut). "Only staff seats, never revenue/booking caps" is a clean wedge.
5. **Deposits are always paywalled** at higher tiers (Meety $47, Apntly Pro, Adva
   EasyAB Pro Plus). Offering it earlier is a hook.
6. **No-show protection** is limited to reminders. No "No-Shield" (auto-charge
   / deposit retention / strike system / waitlist auto-fill) as a differentiated flow.
7. **Onboarding is manual.** "Convert product in 3 steps" but no guided setup wizard
   that produces a working widget + first booking in under 2 minutes.
8. **Support quality is the moat** merchants actually cite. We can't win on features
   alone; we must match fast human support from day one.

## Strategic read
- This is a MATURE, crowded category with 5+ strong incumbents, all Built-for-Shopify,
  all 4.7–5.0 stars, all with free tiers. Feature parity is achievable but expensive.
- Winning wedge must be POSITIONING, not feature count:
  **Staff-first ("book the person, not the slot") + staff performance + fair pricing.**
- Target niche where "staff matters": salons, barbers, tattoo, physio, personal
  trainers, dentists, estheticians — businesses whose customers have a preferred person.
- The 6-8 week scope is realistic ONLY if we cut v1.0 to: staff-first widget +
  booking (no payment) + calendar sync + reminders + deposits + reschedule/cancel.
  Everything else (waitlist, SMS, multi-location, POS) is v1.1+.
